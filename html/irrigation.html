Irrigation Systems in Jordan

Advanced water management techniques essential for agriculture in one of the world's most water-scarce countries.

Drip Irrigation

95% Efficient

Most efficient system used extensively in Jordan Valley. Water is delivered directly to plant roots through emitters.

  • 90-95% water efficiency
  • Reduced evaporation losses
  • Precise nutrient delivery
  • Suitable for all terrains

Sprinkler Irrigation

75% Efficient

Used for field crops and larger agricultural areas, simulating natural rainfall patterns.

  • Good for large areas
  • Uniform water distribution
  • Can apply fertilizers
  • Moderate investment cost

Surface Irrigation

60% Efficient

Traditional method still used in some areas, particularly for flood-tolerant crops.

  • Low initial investment
  • Simple operation
  • Good for rice cultivation
  • Traditional knowledge
